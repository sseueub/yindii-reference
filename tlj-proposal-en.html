01 · About Yindii & Fit with Tous Les Jours
About Yindii

Yindii is a food surplus platform connecting F&B stores with nearby consumers. The Surprise Bag model bundles end-of-day unsold items into a discounted pickup bag — generating incremental revenue from inventory that would otherwise be discarded. No delivery operations required.

  • Pickup-based — no delivery logistics
  • New customer acquisition channel
  • Founded in Thailand · Operating in Singapore & Hong Kong
  • Supports ESG commitments on food waste reduction
Why Tous Les Jours
  • Daily bakery surplus aligns well with the Surprise Bag model
  • Benchmark data available from Tous Les Jours Singapore (3 stores) — useful as a reference for Korea
  • Nationwide franchise network supports potential scaling after the pilot
  • Aligns with CJ Group's ESG direction on food waste reduction

04 · Operations & Pilot Terms
How It Works
Step 1 Store staff list end-of-day surplus items in the Yindii app. Setup support is available from the Yindii team.
Step 2 Each store sets its own pickup window. No delivery operations required.
Step 3 Customers purchase via app and collect in-store within the designated window.
Step 4 Commission applies to completed transactions only.
Pilot Terms
Monthly Fee No platform fee during the pilot period
Commission 20% on completed sales
Fulfillment Pickup-based only — no delivery logistics
Support Staff manual, onboarding support, and store visits from Yindii team
